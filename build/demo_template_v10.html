What this is, and what it is not

RealCalculated for this demo from the licensed survey files in the EGRISS data lake. Only area totals leave the file; no household record is shown.
NoteThese surveys release area names, not coordinates. Camp dots are placed at UNHCR's public site locations, taken from the country card.
To confirmThe surveys were designed for national or stratum estimates. Each cell shows its sample of households; cells under 50 are marked. Confidence intervals are not calculated yet.
NoteA cluster-dot version needs a survey that releases shifted coordinates. Malawi DHS 2024 is in the data lake, but its GPS file is not; it has to be requested from the DHS Program.
